where these numbers come from
  • FanDuel prices: TSN, Nov 13. Kalshi: Covers, Nov 11. Polymarket: On3, after Week 10. Sportsbook average: SportsBettingDime, Nov 9.
  • Season stats: ESPN game logs, Sports-Reference and game reports through Week 10. Team records from the Week 11 AP poll and game reports.
  • Fan vote: Heisman Trust graphics posted on X (Week 10 leaderboard Nov 10, top three Nov 12).
  • Writers' poll: Last Word on Sports, after Week 10.
  • Buzz on X: X search for Heisman posts with 100+ likes, limited to team, media and reporter accounts. Counts were captured when the list was built; summaries are ours.
  • Photos: original images generated with Figma AI for this page; they show no real players.
  • This site is a snapshot. The numbers live in the data/ folder as JSON; update those files to refresh the page.
